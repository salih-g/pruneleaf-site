Pruneleaf

Free the space Claude leaves on your Mac. Keep every Claude Code session safe in iCloud.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store · macOS 26 or later

One-click cleanup

See the VM image, caches and old versions Claude keeps, with every file listed. What you pick goes to the Trash, never deleted outright.

Encrypted iCloud backup

Claude Code deletes old sessions on its own. Pruneleaf keeps them in your private iCloud, encrypted on your Mac with a key only you hold.

Restore after a reinstall

Pick the Mac to restore from, review every file, and bring your sessions back, without overwriting anything already there.
